STREETS OF THE SUN

Milo Reyes came back to Palmera Bay with an empty wallet and a debt to the wrong people. Carmen Solis, the fixer who runs the marina bars, has six jobs for him — steal, deliver, collect, escape, one last big score, and a sunset street race across the whole city. The whole city is open: its avenues, its beach, its traffic, its traffic lights, and its police — and by night the streetlights come on and the heat gets worse. How Milo gets through it is up to you.
